# Sample itinerary planning notes for Singapore

Singapore itineraries work best when each day has a theme and nearby attractions are grouped together. A three-day first-time itinerary can balance modern landmarks, heritage neighbourhoods, food and family-friendly experiences.

## Three-day first-time itinerary pattern

Day 1 can focus on Marina Bay and the Civic District. Suitable activities include Merlion Park, Marina Bay waterfront, Gardens by the Bay, ArtScience Museum, National Gallery Singapore and an evening light show. If the weather is dry, outdoor waterfront walking and Supertree Grove work well. If it rains, use indoor museums, malls and conservatories.

Day 2 can focus on heritage and food. Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Glam can be combined if the traveller enjoys cultural neighbourhoods, temples, murals, markets and food. This day should include rest stops because walking in humid weather can be tiring. Maxwell Food Centre, Tekka Centre or Arab Street/Kampong Glam cafes can be used as food stops.

Day 3 can focus on either Sentosa or family/nature. Families may choose Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, beaches and cable car experiences. Nature and wildlife travellers may choose Singapore Botanic Gardens or Mandai wildlife attractions. Shopping-focused travellers may choose Orchard Road, Jewel Changi Airport or Bugis.

## Family itinerary ideas

Families with children can include Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, Bird Paradise, Gardens by the Bay, S.E.A. Aquarium, Universal Studios Singapore, Jewel Changi Airport, Science Centre Singapore and easy food stops. It is better to avoid overloading the day with too many museums or long heritage walks for younger children.

## Culture itinerary ideas

A culture-focused plan can include Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam, National Gallery Singapore, Asian Civilisations Museum, Peranakan Museum, heritage food and local markets. The plan should include temple etiquette and time for food experiences.

## Food itinerary ideas

Food-focused travellers can plan hawker centres and neighbourhood meals around the sightseeing route. Chinatown and Maxwell can pair with heritage walks. Little India pairs with Tekka Centre and Indian food. Lau Pa Sat can pair with Marina Bay. Katong/Joo Chiat can add Peranakan culture and laksa.
