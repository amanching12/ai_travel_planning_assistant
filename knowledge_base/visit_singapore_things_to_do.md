# Things to do in Singapore

Singapore offers attractions across nature, culture, food, shopping, entertainment and family experiences. The best plan depends on traveller type, weather and available time.

## Outdoor activity suggestions

Outdoor-friendly options include Marina Bay waterfront, Gardens by the Bay outdoor gardens, Singapore Botanic Gardens, Fort Canning, Southern Ridges, Sentosa beaches, Haji Lane street art walks, Chinatown heritage walks and Kampong Glam streets. These activities are better in the morning, late afternoon or evening because the weather can be hot and humid.

## Indoor activity suggestions

Indoor and rain-friendly options include National Gallery Singapore, Asian Civilisations Museum, ArtScience Museum, Gardens by the Bay conservatories, S.E.A. Aquarium, Jewel Changi Airport, shopping malls, indoor food trails and selected family attractions. These work well when rain or high heat makes outdoor walking uncomfortable.

## Neighbourhood recommendations

For modern landmarks, choose Marina Bay. For Chinese heritage and food, choose Chinatown. For Indian culture and markets, choose Little India. For Malay-Arab heritage and cafes, choose Kampong Glam. For shopping and malls, choose Orchard Road and Bugis. For beaches and theme attractions, choose Sentosa. For nature and wildlife, choose Botanic Gardens or Mandai.

## Traveller-fit recommendations

First-time visitors should prioritise Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown, Kampong Glam or Little India, and either Sentosa or a museum/food trail depending on interest.

Families should prioritise Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa, S.E.A. Aquarium, Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, Bird Paradise and Jewel Changi Airport.

Culture-focused travellers should prioritise Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam, National Gallery Singapore, Asian Civilisations Museum and Peranakan-related experiences.

Budget-conscious travellers can use MRT and buses, hawker centres, free outdoor districts, parks, waterfront walks and selected free light shows.
