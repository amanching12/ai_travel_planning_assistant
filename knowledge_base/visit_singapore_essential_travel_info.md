# Essential travel information for Singapore

Singapore has a tropical climate. Visitors should expect warm and humid conditions throughout the year, with possible rain even when the day starts sunny. A practical daily plan should include water, comfortable shoes, light clothing, sun protection and backup indoor options.

English is widely used in Singapore, and Singapore's official languages include English, Malay, Mandarin and Tamil. Most tourists can navigate transport, restaurants and attractions comfortably in English.

Singapore is highly connected. Tourist areas generally have good mobile connectivity, and visitors commonly use local SIM, eSIM or roaming options. Navigation, transport planning and cashless payment apps can be useful.

Visitors should check official entry requirements before travelling. Many visitors must complete required arrival or immigration processes before entering Singapore. Requirements may depend on nationality and travel purpose, so travellers should rely on official government and airline information for final checks.

## Payments and local behaviour

Cards and digital payments are common, but some hawker stalls and small shops may prefer cash or local QR payment methods. Keeping a small amount of Singapore dollars is practical for food courts, markets and backup situations.

Singapore has strict laws and public rules. Visitors should be mindful of smoking restrictions, littering rules, public transport etiquette and local customs. Respectful behaviour is expected in religious sites, heritage areas and residential neighbourhoods.

## Weather-aware travel guidance

Outdoor activities such as Gardens by the Bay outdoor gardens, Marina Bay waterfront walks, Botanic Gardens, Sentosa beaches and heritage walking trails are better when the weather is dry. Indoor alternatives include the ArtScience Museum, National Gallery Singapore, Asian Civilisations Museum, Jewel Changi Airport, Gardens by the Bay conservatories, malls, S.E.A. Aquarium and indoor food trails.
